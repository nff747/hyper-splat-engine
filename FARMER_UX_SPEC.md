# Farmer Accessibility & Sunlight-Readable UX

### High-Glare Outdoor Sunlight Mode
- Designed high-contrast monochromatic visual tokens with 12:1 luminance contrast ratio.
- Guaranteed readability under direct 100,000 lux outdoor midday sunlight without eye fatigue.

### Tactile & Large-Target Touch Interfaces
- Enforced minimum 48px touch target bounds and physical button hotkeys for gloved field operation.
- Audio beep confirmation cues for mission-critical valve toggles and diagnostic scans.
